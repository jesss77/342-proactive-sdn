# Results

## Final paper source of truth

The final paper's [four figures, six tables and source ledger](paper/) are
generated by `analysis/build_paper_assets.py` and `analysis/plot_paper_evidence.py`.
[Rebuild and audit instructions](../docs/PAPER_ASSETS.md) map every asset to its
code and inputs. These combined-paper assets keep V1 raw-backed results and V2
reported summaries separate. The older V1 figures and tables below are retained
as the original-cohort evidence.

## Main comparison

[`matched/trials.csv`](matched/trials.csv) lists all 36 original independent
network trials. [`matched/matched-report.json`](matched/matched-report.json)
contains the accepted inspections and grouped statistics. The columns retain
actual sender/receiver rates, loss, received-probe RTT, actions, denials and costs.

The main finding is unchanged: earlier rerouting improved delivery relative to
fixed routing, but the Decision Tree did not consistently improve networking
outcomes beyond simple trend early warning. One ML low-load switch was
unnecessary. Fixed-alternate variable controls delivered every sender datagram.

Warning episodes are assessed on six held-out fixed-primary traces. ML warned
before 2/3 scorable ramp and 9/25 scorable burst events. Its 24 unmatched episodes
include 20 late and four other alarms; five tail episodes are censored. Under the
scorable-opportunity denominator, unmatched rates are 2.963/min ramp and 5.944/min
bursty. These are offline episodes, not live reroutes; live trials permit only
one successful switch. A high row F1 does not establish useful event warning or
better networking performance.

## Failure and scope evidence

The main 36 trials remain separate from supplemental capacity controls,
diagnostics, the single capture bracket and the incomplete extra cohort. Only
seed 51 completed four policies. Seed 52 reactive/trend passed individually; fixed
failed collector quality before ML. Nine cells were not acquired. The last
failure was one shared polling deadline miss, not four independent failures.
Earlier recovery also failed endpoint-buffer and collector screens.

Raw records for these outcomes remain in `data/inputs.zip`; the default rebuild
re-inspects them and reproduces their success/failure decisions. Intermittent
failure cause is unresolved. [Methods and limitations](../docs/METHODS.md).
